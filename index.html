Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

PrankLab is developed and operated by Balázs Bognár, operating under the name Milbella ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the PrankLab Android application handles information in its current version.

Information stored on your device

PrankLab stores the following app data locally on your device so its features can work:

  • prank names, rules, schedules, enabled or paused states, and execution history;
  • sound selections, favorites, and related preferences;
  • Smart Credit balance and locally cached PRO entitlement status; and
  • app settings and consent preferences.

This information is stored in the app's private Android storage. PrankLab does not currently upload this locally stored content to a PrankLab or Milbella server, and the app does not provide user accounts or cloud synchronization.

Google Play services and purchases

PrankLab includes Google Play Billing functionality for checking and restoring purchase entitlement and for supporting future in-app purchases. Google may process information such as your Google account, device information, purchase status, and transaction details under Google's own privacy terms. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Information we do not currently collect

The current version of PrankLab does not:

  • collect precise or approximate location;
  • access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files;
  • use advertising or the Android Advertising ID;
  • use analytics or behavioral tracking; or
  • use remote crash-reporting services.

Permissions and background operation

PrankLab may receive the Android system's boot-completed event so that locally configured schedules can be restored after the device restarts. This does not give PrankLab access to personal files or communications and does not send your prank configuration to us.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Because PrankLab does not currently transmit locally created prank data to us, we do not share that content with third parties. Google Play may process data independently when providing app distribution, licensing, and billing services.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored PrankLab data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling PrankLab removes its private local data from the device according to Android's normal behavior. As PrankLab does not currently provide an account or store this content on our servers, there is no separate server-side account data to delete.

Security

PrankLab relies on Android's private application storage and platform security controls to protect locally stored data. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, but we limit data handling to what is required for the app's current functionality.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PrankLab's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy takes effect. Material changes will be disclosed as required by applicable rules and platform policies.
